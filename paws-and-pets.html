01 — About us

More personality. Less boring.

Your dog isn't beige. Their accessories shouldn't be either.

Paws & Pets started with a simple, slightly dramatic belief: dog accessories should be as expressive and individual as the dogs wearing them. So we design collars, bandanas and bow ties in bold colour combos and cheeky prints — then make them comfy enough for every zoomie, puddle and dramatic sit-down-mid-walk.

No boring basics. No scratchy bits. Just good stuff that makes strangers stop you on walks to ask, “where did you get that?!”
